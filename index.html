Privacy Policy

Decant: Water Sort Puzzle · Last updated 8 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Decant: Water Sort Puzzle ("the game"), published on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone, under the identifier com.sortlab.watersortpuzzle on both.

The short version

The game has no accounts of its own and no server of its own. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number or location.

  • Both iPhone and Android: ads are shown through Google AdMob, and the optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by the store and recorded through RevenueCat. Both are described below.
  • iPhone: leaderboards use Apple Game Center.
  • Android: Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage statistics) and Google Play Games (optional sign-in and leaderboards).

Information the game stores on your device

Your progress is saved in the app's own private storage on your phone:

  • the level you have reached, and the puzzle you are part-way through
  • your coin balance
  • whether sound is switched on
  • the date you last claimed the daily bonus, and how many bonus videos you have watched today
  • whether you have bought Remove Ads

This stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us and we cannot read it. Uninstalling the game deletes it permanently.

Information collected by advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. On iPhone, the game first asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads are still shown but the Advertising Identifier is not shared. To do that, the AdMob software in the app collects certain information and sends it to Google and its advertising partners:

WhatWhy
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier on your phone; on iPhone, only if you allow tracking) To select ads and limit how often the same ad is repeated
Device information, such as model, operating system version and language To serve an ad that fits your device
Approximate location, derived from your IP address To serve region-appropriate ads and meet local legal requirements
Interactions with ads, such as views and taps To measure advertising and pay the developer

We do not receive any of this ourselves. It is handled by Google under its own policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment; we never see your card or account details. The purchase is recorded through RevenueCat against an anonymous identifier created by the app, so that it can be restored on a new device. RevenueCat's policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Usage statistics (Android only)

The Android version uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the game is played, so we can see which levels are too hard and fix problems. It records events such as starting or finishing a level (with the level number, moves and stars), using a hint or other help, and watching a reward video, together with device information (model, operating system version, language, approximate region from your IP address) and an app instance identifier. It does not include your name, email address or precise location. We see these statistics only as totals. Google processes them under the policy linked above.

Google Play Games (Android only)

If you have a Google Play Games profile, the game signs you in to it automatically and sends your highest level and total stars to its leaderboards, where other players can see them next to your Play Games name. This is handled by Google under its own policy; you can manage your profile and its visibility in the Google Play Games app.

Game Center (iPhone only)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your highest level and total stars to its leaderboards, where other players can see them next to your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under its own policy; you can manage Game Center in the Settings app.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID (Android). On most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads. You can reset the identifier, or delete it so apps can no longer use it to personalise ads.
  • Tracking (iPhone). Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking lets you allow or deny tracking for the game at any time.
  • Turn off ad personalisation (Android). The same screen has a switch for this. Ads will still appear, but they will not be tailored to you.
  • Remove everything. Uninstalling the game deletes all locally stored progress.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. It is intended for players aged 13 and over.

Security

Data sent by the advertising and purchase services travels over encrypted connections. Since the game itself holds no accounts and stores nothing on a server, there is no account of yours for us to lose.
